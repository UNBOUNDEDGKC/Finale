The Finale



As you leave the building, you get a feeling of relief

Of course, because the plot demands it, you notice a trapdoor in the corner of your eye.

Just when you were about to leave, a helicopter appears. Inside, you see the owner of the company, looking very mad.

Understandable, I suppose. Still, his upcoming downfall is absolutely deserved.

You remember the trapdoor, as if you didn't JUST notice it 2 seconds ago.

You run towards it, hearing behind you the sound of gunshots and bullets landing behind you.

You barely make it to the trapdoor.
